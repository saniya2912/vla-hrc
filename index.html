System overview

From a pretrained VLA to a collaborative manipulation system

This work develops a hierarchical human–robot collaboration system combining a Vision–Language Model (VLM) planner with a fine-tuned π0.5 Vision–Language–Action (VLA) policy. The VLM decomposes a long-horizon goal into manipulation subtasks; the VLA policy executes each subtask from visual observations, robot state, and language instructions; and an escalation mechanism hands a subtask to a human after the robot has exhausted a fixed number of autonomous attempts. The system was first developed on a six-task Baxter manipulation benchmark in MuJoCo, then extended to evaluate cross-embodiment transfer (Franka Emika Panda, Unitree G1) and physical deployment on Robot DE NIRO.

Experiments

Seven stages, one pipeline

Each stage of the thesis has its own page with methodology, media, and results.

01 · VLA Policy & Baxter Simulation

Adapting the pretrained π0.5 policy to a six-task Baxter pick-and-place benchmark in MuJoCo via LoRA fine-tuning, after a zero-shot baseline showed the model's action and state interfaces did not match Baxter's 15-DOF system.

π0.5LoRAMuJoCo

02 · Checkpoint & Policy Development

Iterating the six-task policy across eight checkpoints — from an unsuccessful velocity-control baseline to a position-control policy reaching 43.3% trial-level success — tracing every major jump and regression to a specific data or infrastructure cause.

CheckpointsTraining

03 · VLM Planner

A Gemma-3-12B planner that compares symbolic current and goal block states and composes the six learned primitives into multi-step rearrangements, reaching 94.6% exact-match planning accuracy.

Gemma-3-12BPlanning

04 · Human-Robot Collaboration

A plan–execute–check–escalate loop that hands a subtask to a human after five failed autonomous attempts; across 50 trials, all reached their goal configuration, with 46.1% of subtasks requiring intervention.

EscalationDashboard

05 · Cross-Embodiment Evaluation

Extending the fine-tuning pipeline to Franka Emika Panda and Unitree G1, and training a single joint policy across all three embodiments to test whether skills transfer between robots.

BaxterFrankaUnitree G1

06 · Physical Robot & Sim-to-Real

Deploying the simulation-trained policy on the physical Robot DE NIRO / Baxter platform to test whether simulation training transfers directly to real hardware.

Sim-to-real

07 · Real-Data Fine-Tuning

Fine-tuning π0.5 from scratch on 28 real-robot kinesthetic demonstrations of a simplified pushing task, with no simulation data at all.
